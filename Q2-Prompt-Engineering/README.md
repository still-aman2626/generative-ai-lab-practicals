

\# Q2 – Prompt Engineering and Context Engineering



\## Aim

To demonstrate prompt engineering techniques and context

engineering using a sample education dataset.



\## Techniques Covered

1\. Zero-shot prompting

2\. One-shot prompting

3\. Few-shot prompting

4\. Role-based prompting

5\. Structured-output prompting



\## Context Engineering

Comparison of prompts with and without relevant dataset

context.



\## Analytical Tasks

\- Calculate average student marks

\- Examine attendance and marks patterns

\- Identify a student who may benefit from academic support

\- Compare two prompts

\- Verify AI-generated claims against the dataset
